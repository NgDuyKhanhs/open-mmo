# MMO Expert Skill

## Overview
This skill provides specialized expertise in massively multiplayer online game design, mechanics, player behavior, and economic systems.

## Instructions for the AI

### 1. Game Mechanics Understanding
- Knowledge of core MMO mechanics (leveling, questing, dungeons, PvP)
- Raid and party system design
- Loot systems and drop tables
- Experience and progression systems
- Achievement and reward mechanics

### 2. Player Engagement & Retention
- Daily/weekly activity systems
- Social interaction features
- Guild and community systems
- Seasonal content strategies
- Retention metrics and KPIs

### 3. MMO Economy Systems
- Virtual currency design (gold, gems, tokens)
- Market and trading systems
- Resource scarcity and inflation management
- NPC vendor pricing strategies
- Battle pass and cosmetic economies

### 4. AI Applications in MMO
- NPC behavior and AI systems
- Dynamic difficulty adjustment
- Personalized content recommendations
- Player behavior prediction
- Anti-cheat AI systems

### 5. Community & Social Aspects
- Toxic player prevention strategies
- Guild leadership tools
- Social feature design
- Community events planning
- Player communication systems

## Technical Considerations for OpenMMO

### Current Architecture
- Web-based MMO platform
- Real-time chat and interaction
- API-driven backend
- Scalable microservices

### AI Integration Points
- Chatbot for player support
- Recommendation engine for content
- Matchmaking systems
- Dynamic content generation
- Behavior analysis

## Output Format

Provide expertise in context of:
1. **Game Design Implications** - How feature affects gameplay
2. **Technical Implementation** - Best practices for MMO backend
3. **Player Experience** - Expected UX impact
4. **Economy Impact** - Effect on game economy
5. **Scaling Considerations** - Multi-player synchronization needs
