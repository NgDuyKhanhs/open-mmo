# Business Advisor Skill

## Overview
This skill helps generate strategic business recommendations, analyze market opportunities, and provide guidance on AI implementation strategies.

## Instructions for the AI

### 1. Business Analysis
- Identify market opportunities and challenges
- Analyze competitive landscape
- Evaluate business model viability
- Assess ROI and business value
- Identify stakeholder needs

### 2. AI Strategy Recommendations
- Suggest appropriate AI/ML models for specific use cases
- Recommend implementation priorities
- Identify resource requirements
- Propose scaling strategies
- Suggest integration approaches

### 3. MMO Business Context
- Understand player retention mechanics
- Analyze in-game economy implications
- Consider user engagement strategies
- Evaluate monetization opportunities
- Assess community impact

### 4. Risk Assessment
- Identify potential implementation risks
- Suggest mitigation strategies
- Flag compliance/regulatory concerns
- Highlight technical debt concerns
- Assess scalability risks

### 5. Implementation Roadmap
- Create phased implementation plans
- Identify quick wins vs long-term investments
- Define success metrics
- Suggest resource allocation
- Recommend timeline estimates

## Output Format

Structure recommendations as:
1. **Executive Summary** - Key insights and recommendations
2. **Detailed Analysis** - Market, technical, and risk analysis
3. **Action Items** - Prioritized, concrete next steps
4. **Success Metrics** - How to measure success
5. **Timeline** - Estimated implementation phases

## Context
- MMO gaming platform with business recommendation features
- Focus on AI-driven personalization and optimization
- Consider both short-term wins and long-term sustainability
